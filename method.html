BEHIND THE FORECAST

Past matches.
Future possibilities.

Matchday is a personal football prediction project. A trained Poisson model turns previous match results into estimated probabilities for upcoming games.

The inputs

Team and competition identities, earlier goals and results are used to calculate 56 inputs, including recent form, team strength, venue history and rest days. Shots and bookmaker odds are not used.

The timing

Only results already observed and accepted at prediction time can be used. There is also a two-calendar-day match-date delay. Future results cannot enter the features.

The three outcomes

H means home win, D means draw and A means away win after 90 minutes plus stoppage time. Extra time and penalties are not part of these predictions. The three probabilities add to 100%.

The double chances

1X combines home and draw, X2 combines draw and away, and 12 combines home and away. Each is the sum of its two outcomes. These choices overlap, so their probabilities do not add to 100%.

The data checks

Unverified identities, conflicting results, stale feeds and started matches are held back. Limited team history is flagged. The page pauses upcoming forecasts when its feed expires.

The limitations

The model estimates patterns in historical results. It does not know everything about injuries, lineups or events between data updates. Higher probability does not guarantee an outcome or profit.

Read the timestamps

Generated is when the forecast was calculated. Data observed is when the source feed was last retrieved. Model fitted is the model’s training date. Opening or refreshing this website does not retrain the model or fetch live football results.

Updates are scheduled twice daily, at 06:17 and 18:17 Tanzania time. Other historical league CSVs are checked on Monday and Thursday, subject to source cooldowns. Scheduled runs may be delayed; use the displayed observation time to judge freshness. Only the Premier League has an enabled upcoming-match feed. Other historical competitions are not automatically presented as forecast coverage.

New results can change form and ratings while the fitted model stays unchanged. A replacement model must pass its release checks before being used. The current model’s validity is enforced by the feed.
