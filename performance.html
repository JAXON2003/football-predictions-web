What will be measured

Probability quality

How well the full home, draw and away probabilities match actual results, using log loss and Brier score.

Outcome accuracy

How often the selected outcome happened, with match counts and separate double-chance results.

Coverage & timing

Which matches had eligible forecasts and when they were first verified publicly available.

A fair timing rule

The intended primary scoring rule uses the latest eligible forecast verified public at least two hours before kickoff, once the actual kickoff and final regulation result are confirmed. A forecast saved privately does not count as publicly published. Missing or late forecasts will be reported separately.

Training evaluation and future public results are different evidence. We will not present historical test scores as this website’s live success rate.
