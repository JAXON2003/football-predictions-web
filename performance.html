Do draw probabilities match reality?

For example, forecasts near 30% draw probability should produce draws about 30% of the time over a large sample. Small groups fluctuate.

Each double-chance market

Outcome frequency describes how often each combination occurred across all scored matches; it is not the selected-pick success rate. Binary Brier measures that combination’s probability quality. Double chances cover two outcomes, so their success rate is naturally higher.

The timing and correction rules

For each fixture, we use the latest generated forecast verified publicly available at least two hours before the provider’s final recorded kickoff. Private forecasts do not count. The API records scheduled kickoff; it does not independently confirm the exact moment play began. Observed rescheduling requires a fresh forecast for the revised kickoff.

Only accepted final regulation scores count, including stoppage time. Each fixture counts once. Each competition’s public tracking begins with its own first verified forecast publication; earlier matches are not backfilled into performance. Disputed results leave the current totals until resolved; verified corrections update the totals, while earlier report snapshots remain in the private audit trail. All forecast versions are retained.

Training evaluation and this public track record are separate. These figures do not measure betting profit and do not guarantee future results. Result checks follow the twice-daily update schedule, so completed matches can remain pending until the next successful collection.
